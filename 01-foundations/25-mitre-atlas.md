# Bài 25: MITRE ATLAS — AI Threat Intelligence Framework

## Overview

MITRE ATLAS (Adversarial Threat Landscape for AI Systems):
- **16 tactics, 84 techniques, 56 sub-techniques**
- **32 mitigations, 42 case studies**
- Updated Feb 2026: added agentic AI techniques
- Complements OWASP LLM Top 10 and NIST AI RMF

## Attack Lifecycle

Reconnaissance → Resource Development → Initial Access → Execution → Persistence → Privilege Escalation → Defense Evasion → Collection → Exfiltration → Impact

## ATLAS + OpenClaw Investigation (Feb 2026)

MITRE analyzed OpenClaw incidents → discovered **new techniques** for AI-first ecosystems:
- AI agents that independently make decisions
- Take actions without continuous human oversight
- New exploit execution paths unique to AI systems

## How to Use ATLAS

- **Security analysis** of AI systems
- **AI development** and implementation guidance
- **Threat assessments** for AI deployments
- **Red-teaming** and reporting attacks
- **Gap analysis** against existing controls
