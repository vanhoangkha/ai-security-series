# Bài 14: AI Governance & EU AI Act 2026 — Compliance Landscape

## EU AI Act Timeline (Updated 2026)

| Date | Milestone |
|------|-----------|
| Aug 2024 | AI Act entered into force |
| Feb 2025 | Prohibited AI practices take effect |
| Aug 2026 | High-risk AI requirements (DELAYED by Omnibus) |
| Dec 2026 | Watermarking obligations for AI content |
| Dec 2027 | Rules for biometrics, critical infrastructure |
| Aug 2028 | Rules for AI in regulated products |

## AI Omnibus (May 7, 2026)

EU institutions đạt deal — amendments đầu tiên kể từ adoption June 2024:

**Changes:**
- Timeline relief cho industry
- Targeted simplification
- New prohibitions (ban "nudifier" apps)
- Watermarking deadline moved up to Dec 2026

**Controversy:**
- EDRi: "Delays key protections, weakens transparency"
- Industry: "Needed flexibility for global AI competitiveness"

## High-Risk AI Classification

Systems classified high-risk nếu:
- Là safety component of a product, HOẶC
- Là product covered by EU harmonization legislation

## Impact on Developers

1. **Transparency requirements** — Document AI system capabilities and limitations
2. **Risk assessment** — Classify your AI system's risk level
3. **Technical documentation** — Maintain detailed records
4. **Human oversight** — Design for human intervention
5. **Data governance** — Training data quality and representativeness
6. **Security by design** — Mandatory cho high-risk systems
